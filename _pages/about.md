I’m **Mohammad Hosein (Cena) Ashoori**. I’m a software engineer and product builder who takes applications from an initial idea through product design, implementation, deployment, and continued improvement, with a focus on **React, Next.js, TypeScript, Python, Django, Flutter, and FastAPI**.

## Recent product work

- **[Abroadin](https://abroadin.com/)** — contributing across product and software engineering, from shaping user-facing workflows and platform capabilities to building and improving the application through production delivery.
- **[DocSphere](https://docsphere.app/)** — building a digital health product and its end-to-end care and service workflows.
- **[Wearto](https://wearto.app/)** — developing an AI-assisted fashion and commerce experience across web and mobile, including virtual try-on.
